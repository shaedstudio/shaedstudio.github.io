You don't need to buy anything to stay safe

Start with the free step under each question. The paid tools are optional, for people who want more.

Sent money or shared bank details?

  1. Call your bank now. Dial 159 to reach your bank's fraud team. If your bank isn't covered, use the number on the back of your card.
  2. Report it. Use Report Fraud or call 0300 123 2040. In Scotland, call Police Scotland on 101.

Lost nothing, but something looks off?

Text message
Forward it to 7726. It's free.
Email
Forward it to report@phishing.gov.uk.

Four questions to check today

The safety steps follow advice from the UK's National Cyber Security Centre, which doesn't endorse the products named here. No tool can stop every scam, so if a message rushes you, stop and check first.

Could someone get into your email with just a password?

Free

Turn on two-step verification for your email first, because it can be used to reset your other accounts. An authenticator app or passkey is stronger than a text code, but a text code is much better than nothing.

Optional

A YubiKey is a small hardware key you plug in or tap to sign in. If you buy one, get a spare so a lost key doesn't lock you out.

Do you use the same password in more than one place?

Free

A password manager can create and remember a different password for every account. These free plans include unlimited logins and passkey support:

  • Proton Pass
  • Bitwarden
Optional

Proton Pass paid plans lift the free plan's limits on built-in sign-in codes and hide-my-email aliases.

Would you lose your photos if your computer died today?

Free

Keep a copy of what matters somewhere else. If you use an external drive, unplug it when the backup finishes so malware can't reach it as easily.

Prefer the cloud? IDrive Basic gives you 10 GB free, with no card needed.

Optional

IDrive's paid plans are for when 10 GB isn't enough.

Is your phone or computer still getting security updates?

Free

Turn on automatic updates. If your device no longer gets them, plan to replace it.

Windows comes with Windows Security built in. Bitdefender Antivirus Free is another no-cost option for Windows.

Optional

Bitdefender's paid versions add features the free one leaves out. Check you need them before paying.
